About Us

Fits like a Glove (FLAG) is a social organization for people who enjoy wearing and celebrating gloves, leather and uniforms in many aspects of their lives. We hold events, meetings and social gatherings for our members, and anyone who is curious or interested is always welcome to attend.

We have a diverse membership and do not discriminate against anyone because of race, color, religion or national origin, sex or sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, or disabilities.

FLAG started in 1991, and continues to be an active part of the leather and fetish community worldwide. We're always happy to work with other leather or fetish people and groups, and want to build a broader and healthier community. Our belief is that all people should be treated with love, honor and respect.
